## Has Uniswap Lost Retail Investors to Solana?

---

## Executive Summary: The Verdict

**YES - Uniswap HAS lost significant retail market share to Solana DEXs**, but it's more nuanced than simple replacement:

- **2025 Shift**: Solana DEX volume surpassed Ethereum/Uniswap in early 2025 through August
- **August 2025 Reversal**: Ethereum reclaimed leadership (institutional interest driven)
- **User Base**: Solana attracts more RETAIL traders; Uniswap maintains institutional dominance

The story: **Retail fled to Solana. Institutions stayed with Uniswap.**
